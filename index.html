Auto log journeys

Listening starts live GPS so the speedo updates immediately. After you stay parked ~2 minutes it drops to sparse checks, then resumes when you are moving again. Journeys start on the road and end after you park. Tap the speed display to retry GPS.

Alarm is a single soft ding while over the active limit (mapped stretch or your Set limit). Silence mutes until you drop back under (or tap Silence again).

Set limit sticks along the route. Mapped limits from earlier trips take over when you enter them. Changing mid-trip only overrides until that mapped stretch ends.

Average zones: tap Avg zone at the start, Exit avg zone at the end. Your average flashes and dings every 5s if over the avg limit.
